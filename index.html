Download or print

For online job portals (ATS), use Print or Save as PDF or the Word file, because they keep your text readable by software. Download PDF gives an image-perfect copy for email. When printing, turn on Background graphics.

My saved CVs

Keep one version per job type, for example "GIS Engineer — UAE" and "Remote Sensing — Research". Everything is stored only in this browser. Save a backup file from the menu to move it to another device.

AI settings

The AI writer, bullet rewriter and CV review use Claude through your own Anthropic API key. Everything else works without one.

Your key is stored only in this browser and is sent only to api.anthropic.com. Don't use this on a shared computer.
